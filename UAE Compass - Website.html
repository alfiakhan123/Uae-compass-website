UAE Compass is built for every UAE resident — Emirati or expat, whether you arrived last month or have lived here for years. From understanding UAE rules and getting around, to finding a home, comparing everyday services, trading locally and sending money home — UAE Compass brings the things every resident needs together in one convenient platform.

Rules & Laws

Know the Rules. Avoid the Fines.

UAE laws and regulations can sometimes be unfamiliar, even to long-term residents. Something that may seem like a simple everyday activity can potentially result in a fine.

UAE Compass helps every resident understand important rules and regulations in simple, easy-to-follow language, so they can make informed decisions and avoid unnecessary penalties.

Know before you do.
⚖️
Explore

Explore Every Emirate

The UAE has far more to offer than the handful of places that usually appear at the top of internet searches.

UAE Compass helps you discover indoor and outdoor activities across the UAE, whether you live in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah or another emirate. Find places to visit, things to do and experiences you may never have discovered through a normal search.

Don't just visit the UAE. Explore it.
🧭
Transport

Get Around With Confidence

Getting from one emirate to another, or across a city, shouldn't mean juggling multiple apps and guessing at fares.

UAE Compass helps residents plan trips within and between all seven emirates, so getting around becomes one less thing to think about.

Plan the route, not the guesswork.

Home

Rent or Buy, Without Overpaying

Looking for a shared room, studio or apartment to rent — or ready to buy your own place?

UAE Compass helps you explore both rental and purchase options while providing useful information about typical prices by area. Knowing the going rate in a neighbourhood helps you compare options, negotiate confidently and make better-informed decisions before spending your hard-earned money.

Know the market before you pay.

About the founder

Alfia Khan

Founder, UAE Compass

Alfia holds a Master's degree and brings over 10 years of IT experience, having worked with startups and multinational companies.

After relocating to the UAE, she experienced firsthand the learning curve of settling into a new country — understanding the rules, finding reliable information, navigating everyday processes and discovering everything the UAE has to offer.

She realised that many of these essentials were scattered across different websites, apps and sources.

That experience inspired her to create UAE Compass — one simple platform designed to help people know the rules, settle with confidence, explore the UAE and manage everyday life with ease.

Her vision is simple:

Make life in the UAE easier, more convenient and stress-free — for everyone.
